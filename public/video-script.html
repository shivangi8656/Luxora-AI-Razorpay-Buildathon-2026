01. AI Growth & Agentic Commerce — 5-Minute Video Pitch & Demo Script
Complete Presenter Dialogues, Step-by-Step UI Actions & Evaluation Bar Compliance Guide
Challenge Objective & Evaluation Criteria
Build AI solutions that can increase merchant revenue or enable merchants to become transactable by AI buyers. Every money-related action must be Explainable, Bounded, and Gated. The solution must demonstrate an Audit Trail, at least one Failure Scenario, and Graceful Failure Handling.

Evaluation Bar Compliance Matrix

Explainable
Merchant Copilot provides step-by-step growth forecasts, gross margin impacts, and funnel data before any money action.
Bounded
Discounts, pricing overrides, and inventory limits are bounded strictly by predefined margin guardrails.
Gated (Human-in-the-loop)
Zero autonomous live changes. AI requires human confirmation and 1-click gated deployment.
Audit Trail
Enterprise ledger logs AI proposals, merchant confirmations, coupon toggles, and Razorpay orders with immutable timestamps.
Failure Scenario
Deactivated/Expired privilege re-application and Razorpay test-mode payment gateway decline simulations.
Graceful Recovery
Zero cart loss on decline, clear non-technical explanation, safe 1-click retry without double billing.

5-Minute Step-by-Step Video Script

Minute 0:00 – 0:45 Hook & Dual-Engine Problem Statement
Screen / Action: Start on the Luxora editorial landing page. Showcase the sleek dual toggle: "Buyer" vs "Business".
"Hello judges! Welcome to Luxora — an end-to-end AI Growth and Agentic Commerce platform built for high-value merchants and autonomous AI buyers. Today, e-commerce faces a two-sided challenge: 1. Merchants struggle to spot live revenue leaks and take hours to formulate and deploy safe promotional campaigns. 2. Autonomous AI buyers demand transactable, machine-readable stores where money-related actions are strictly explainable, bounded, and gated. Luxora solves both sides through a unified dual portal: a live Buyer Storefront with Conversational Checkout, paired with an Autonomous Merchant Growth Copilot backed by enterprise audit governance. Let's see it in action!"
Minute 0:45 – 1:45 Merchant Copilot: Natural Language & Revenue Telemetry
Screen / Action: Click header toggle to "Business". Open Merchant Workspace AI Copilot. Click prompt: "Kaha kitna revenue loss ho rha kyu ho rha kaise theek kr sakte?"
"We switch directly into the Merchant Workspace. Our AI Copilot communicates fluently in English, Hindi, and Hinglish. Let's ask a candid merchant inquiry: 'Kaha kitna revenue loss ho rha kyu ho rha kaise theek kr sakte?' Observe how the Copilot responds: It doesn't offer generic advice. It scans live funnel telemetry, isolates cart drop-offs on the Noir Column Dress, and models an exact recovery plan. This demonstrates our first core pillar: Explainability. The AI delivers clear Growth Highlights, Margin Risk analysis, and an explicit action rationale before suggesting any monetary change."
Minute 1:45 – 2:30 Bounded & Gated Execution (Live Storefront Sync)
Screen / Action: Type: "Haan coupon FESTIVE30 pe 30% off active kr do". Highlight the confirmation box, then click "Deploy to Storefront". Show the spinner turning into green "Live & Deployed".
"Now, let's examine the hackathon evaluation bar: • Is this action Bounded? Yes, discount rates are checked against strict gross-margin guardrails. • Is it Gated? Absolutely. The AI is forbidden from pushing promos live autonomously. It presents a gated confirmation panel requiring merchant sign-off. I say: 'Haan coupon FESTIVE30 pe 30% off active kr do'. The AI verifies the rule and prompts for approval. As I click 'Deploy to Storefront', watch the real-time sync: Instant visual feedback — the button transitions into a verified green 'Live & Deployed' state, and a sync alert confirms deployment across the storefront!"
Minute 2:30 – 3:30 Omnichannel Verification & Conversational Checkout
Screen / Action: Toggle back to "Buyer" mode. Point out the top Hero Marquee showing FESTIVE30. Add an item to bag, open Bag Drawer, see FESTIVE30 auto-applied, click Proceed to Checkout.
"Let's verify omnichannel synchronization. We glide back to the Buyer Storefront with zero page reloads. Look at the Hero Coupon Marquee and promotional banner: FESTIVE30 is live and streaming! As a buyer, I add the luxury piece to my bag and slide open the Bag Drawer. The newly deployed FESTIVE30 coupon is automatically recognized, deducting 30% from the order subtotal. When I proceed to checkout, our Conversational Checkout and Razorpay Test-Mode integration initiates, ensuring bounded tokenization and a seamless buying journey."
Minute 3:30 – 4:15 Failure Scenarios & Deterministic Graceful Recovery
Screen / Action: Demo two scenarios: 1. Deactivating a coupon and seeing graceful rejection at checkout. 2. Razorpay test-mode payment gateway decline simulation.
"Now to the most critical hackathon criteria: Failure Scenarios and Graceful Handling. Failure Scenario 1: Deactivated or Expired Privilege. In Merchant AI, if we instruct 'Ye coupon deactivate kar do', the promo is instantly killed from the active registry. If a shopper attempts to apply the old code, the app does not break or crash — it delivers a polite, informative notice: 'This privilege has expired' and auto-recommends current active alternatives. Failure Scenario 2: Payment Gateway Decline. During Razorpay checkout, if a payment is declined or times out, our architecture guarantees zero cart state loss. The items remain securely locked in the bag, a human-readable failure diagnosis is displayed, and a 1-click retry is offered with zero duplicate charge risk."
Minute 4:15 – 5:00 Enterprise Audit Trail Governance & High-Impact Closing
Screen / Action: Navigate to Merchant Workspace -> "Audit Governance" tab. Show logged events with timestamps and JSON payload viewer. Finish on camera or dual view.
"Finally, financial safety is impossible without total accountability. Here is our Enterprise Audit Trail. Every single action is permanently recorded: • The AI's proposed coupon and stock changes. • The merchant's cryptographic approval timestamp. • Razorpay test order IDs, payment statuses, and balance reconciliations. Merchants can inspect raw JSON payloads or export audit reports in one click. To conclude: Luxora transforms passive digital boutiques into Autonomous, High-Converting Commerce Engines. Every dollar-related action is Explainable, Bounded, Gated by human consent, audited end-to-end, and resilient against edge-case failures. Thank you!"
